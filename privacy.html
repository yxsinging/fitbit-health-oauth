Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Purpose

Health Agent is a private tool used solely by its owner to read and summarize the owner's Fitbit and Google Health data.

Data access and use

The app requests read-only access to authorized health and fitness data. Data is used only to produce personal summaries and recommendations requested by the owner.

Sharing and sale

Health data is not sold, used for advertising, or disclosed publicly. The app does not intentionally share personal health data with third parties.

Storage and security

Authorization credentials are kept in the owner's private local environment. Public website files contain no health data or authorization credentials.

Control and revocation

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the security settings of the connected Google account.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through the owner's GitHub profile.
